Overview

What information may be collected.

When visiting this website, certain technical information may be collected automatically for analytics, operational monitoring, and security purposes.

When using the contact form, submitted contact details and message content are stored securely to allow communication and follow-up.

Collected Data

Technical and communication-related information.

Analytics

Visited pages, timestamps, language preferences, and referrer information.

Technical Data

Browser user agent, IP address, and request metadata used for operational monitoring and abuse prevention.

Contact Requests

Name, email address, subject, and message content submitted through the contact form.

Security Logs

Limited request logging may be retained temporarily to detect spam, abuse, unauthorized access attempts, and service issues.

Purpose

How collected information is used.

Collected information is used exclusively for website reliability, traffic analysis, operational monitoring, communication handling, debugging, and security purposes.

Data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for marketing campaigns.

Access to stored information is restricted to the website owner and protected administrative systems.

Retention & Security

Reasonable operational and security practices.

Data Retention

Technical analytics and operational logs may be retained for a limited duration for monitoring, debugging, and security review.

Contact Requests

Contact form submissions may be retained to maintain communication history and respond to ongoing discussions.

Security

Reasonable technical and administrative safeguards are used to protect stored information against unauthorized access or misuse.
